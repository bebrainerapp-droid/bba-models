BB Anatomy 3D - model files (*.bin)

These files are adapted (simplified, reduced in polygon count, re-packed and re-coloured in the viewer) from:

- Z-Anatomy - The libre 3D atlas of anatomy - CC BY-SA 4.0
  https://www.z-anatomy.com  |  https://github.com/LluisV/Z-Anatomy
- BodyParts3D - The Database Center for Life Science - CC BY-SA 2.1 Japan
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html
- Cranial nerves: "Cranial Nerves and Foramina" by University of Dundee, CAHID - CC BY 4.0

The kidney model of Z-Anatomy (CC BY-NC) and the inner-ear model (CC BY-NC-SA) are NOT included.

These adapted model files are distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Changes made: selected structures, merged into named parts, decimated, quantised and packed into a compact binary format for web viewing.
